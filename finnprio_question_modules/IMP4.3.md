### Options
**IMP4.3. Would the pest impact plants which have an important, recognized position in the culture?**

### Guidance
- This is a boolean question answered yes or no.
- Culturally important plants include traditional food plants (e.g., potato), plants in folktales and mythology (e.g., ash in Nordic mythology), and plants with symbolic values (e.g., provincial flowers).
- Consider only host plants threatened in EST1 and EST2.
- If spread depends on vector, consider vector's effect on these impacts.

### Headings

- Impact on traditional food plants
- Impact on plants in folklore and mythology
- Impact on plants with symbolic values
- Vector species considerations
