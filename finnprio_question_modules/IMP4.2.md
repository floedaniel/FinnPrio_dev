## IMP4.2. Would the pest cause significant aesthetic impacts in the PRA area?

### Options
**IMP4.2. Would the pest cause significant aesthetic impacts in the PRA area?**

### Guidance
- This is a boolean question answered yes or no.
- Aesthetic impacts include negative effects on visual perception of gardens, urban areas or landscapes (e.g., Dutch elm disease, horse chestnut leaf miner).
- Consider only host plants threatened in EST1 and EST2.
- If spread depends on vector, consider vector's effect on these impacts.

### Headings

- Aesthetic damage to gardens and urban areas
- Aesthetic damage to landscapes
- Vector species considerations
- Summary and recommended option (yes/no)
