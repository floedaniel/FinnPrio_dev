## IMP2.2. Is the pest a vector for other pests?

### Options
**Yes**
Yes the pest a vector for other pests
**No**
No the pest a not a vector for other pests

### Guidance
- This is a boolean question  answered yes or no.

### Headings

- Vector status of the pest
- Pests vectored by this species
- Summary and recommended option (yes/no)
